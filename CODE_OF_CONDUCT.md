# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
professional and constructive experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience
levels and perspectives.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, data and methodology rather than people.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Clearly communicate uncertainty and limitations.
- Credit prior work and data sources.
- Respect licenses and attribution requirements.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of project infrastructure.
- Misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, data or dependencies.

## Scientific and Technical Conduct

Because this project involves machine learning and statistical analysis,
contributors should make a reasonable effort to:

- Avoid data leakage.
- Report methodology accurately.
- Avoid cherry-picking results.
- Clearly distinguish experiments from established results.
- Document assumptions.
- Report meaningful negative results where relevant.
- Avoid presenting probabilistic predictions as certainty.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate
this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent
exclusion from the project.

## Reporting

For conduct issues, contact the project maintainers privately using the
security/contact mechanism documented by the repository.

For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md`.
